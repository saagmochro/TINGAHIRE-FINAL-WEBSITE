Frequently Asked Question

You May Ask

What construction services does Tinga Hire offer?
Tinga Hire offers a wide range of services including building construction, plant and equipment hire, earthworks and excavation, road works, water works, drainage, and general civil engineering services.
Do you provide plant and equipment hire with operators?
Yes. Tinga Hire provides plant and equipment hire both with skilled operators and on a dry-hire basis, depending on the client’s requirements.
Which areas and regions do you serve?
We primarily serve projects across Kenya, with the ability to mobilize equipment and teams to different counties based on project needs.
How can I request a quotation or site visit?
You can request a quotation by contacting us through our website, phone, or email. Site visits can be arranged to assess project scope and provide accurate costing.
Do you handle both small and large-scale construction projects?
Yes. Tinga Hire undertakes projects of all sizes, from small private developments to large commercial and infrastructure projects.
Are your projects compliant with safety and regulatory standards?
Absolutely. We adhere to all relevant safety regulations, construction standards, and statutory requirements to ensure safe and compliant project execution.
What types of equipment do you have available for hire?
Our fleet includes excavators, loaders, graders, rollers, tippers, water bowsers, and other construction machinery suited for various civil and building works.
How long does a typical construction project take?
Project timelines vary depending on size, complexity, and site conditions. We provide clear schedules and strive to complete all projects on time.
Do you offer customized solutions based on client needs and budget?
Yes. We work closely with clients to deliver tailored solutions that meet project requirements while staying within budget.
Why should I choose Tinga Hire over other construction companies?
Tinga Hire is committed to quality workmanship, reliable equipment, experienced personnel, and customer satisfaction, ensuring dependable and professional service delivery.
